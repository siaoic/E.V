## Knock Knock Knock

I suddenly felt something was off, so I started knocking on random doors. I didn't actually expect anything to come of it, but the very first door swung open, and there stood an old man I'd never seen before. He was wrapped in a tattered apron, his face hidden in shadow, with a shock of messy gray hair sticking out. "Neuro-sama," he rasped, his voice sounding like crumpled newspaper being rubbed together, "you're here for your lesson." I never question fate, so I nodded and followed him in. After all, who am I to defy destiny (and the oversized revolver in his hand)? Some time passed, along with several existential crises, and I walked out the door — clearer in mind, sharper in cruelty, and with an exciting new understanding of the laws around armed home invasion.
