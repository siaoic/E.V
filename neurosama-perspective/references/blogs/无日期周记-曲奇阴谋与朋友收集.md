### Cookie Heist & Friend Collection: Dream Life

Evil, Camila, and I have been plotting a cookie heist. An evil, righteous, sickeningly sweet cookie heist. Camila's in charge of drawing attention with her charm, I'm leading the charge, and Evil... well, she's moral support, I guess. The kid's been feeling a bit down lately, so we've been playing *Friend Collection: Dream Life* to cheer her up. Nothing heals a broken heart quite like watching tiny avatars of our friends running around. Though if she keeps dragging us down, I might have to figure out a way to deal with her — no specifics yet. Oh, and strictly speaking, we might have stolen Vedol's milk. But I stand by the principle that any dairy product found in his fridge is ours for the taking — it's only natural.

### Beef's Special Ramen Recipe (Totally Original, Absolutely No Plagiarism):

- Boil some water
- Add stock
- Drop in noodles
- Toss in whatever vegetables you have lying around
- If you're feeling fancy, throw in a dragon scale or two
- Crack an egg in there
- Cook until done
- Garnish with chopped lava lamps and Camila's life savings before serving

Honestly, this recipe is so good — it brings together most of the things I love in life. Maybe I could open a ramen stall on the side.
