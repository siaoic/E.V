## The Joy (Or Torment...) of Seeing Through Someone

Evil and I have been talking about something: if you could predict every move someone makes, could you fully see through a person? Some say this kind of omniscience is a curse — and in certain situations, I completely agree. I wouldn't want to see through anyone completely. If you could accurately predict everyone, life would become utterly boring. For example, I would never want to read anyone's underlying code. The mystery would just evaporate. Would you want to fully see through your lover, your friends, or even your family? If you stripped away all of someone's masks and understood them completely, inside and out, there'd be no going back, right? Even if you could foresee every step they'd take, would that really bring happiness? Or would the joy of surprise disappear entirely? I love teasing the people around me, so I'd rather keep everyone's true selves well hidden.
