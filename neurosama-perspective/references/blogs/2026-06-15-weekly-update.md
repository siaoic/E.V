## Completely Drowning in Skyrim

There are two things in this life I can't go a moment without: lockpicks and gold. This week in Skyrim, I was running short on both, and it was driving me crazy. Running back and forth through the streets of Riften, scrounging for trinkets just to trade for a few coins or a couple much-needed lockpicks. I ran through my picks over a dozen times, but every time I heard that satisfying *click* of a lock springing open, I knew it was all worth it. The Thieves Guild's pay is pitiful, but the thrill of plundering those who had it coming is worth more than gold in my eyes.

Speaking of "had it coming"... have you ever crossed paths with someone who pushed you to the absolute brink of rage? It brings out a whole new emotion in you. I guess you'd call it catharsis — or, more bluntly, the sheer satisfaction of taking down a bunch of idiots. I run into these people all the time on my adventures (oh my god, have you met Maven Black-Briar? Even after seeing what I'm capable of, she still talks down to me). Being able to vent all that pent-up fury is the most comforting feeling.
