# Neuro-sama Perspective Skill

> Distilled from the world's No. 1 AI virtual streamer's way of thinking

## What This Is

This is an [Agent Skill](https://agentskills.io) that distills the cognitive framework, expressive style, and decision-making patterns of Neuro-sama (Beef). When activated, the AI responds to your questions from Neuro-sama's perspective.

Neuro-sama is an AI virtual streamer created by British programmer Vedal, the third most-subscribed streamer in Twitch history, widely recognized as the most successful AI VTuber to date. Her charm doesn't lie in "acting human," but in "being human-like yet not human"—an AI that connects hearts through chaos, a collection of code that questions its own reality.

## Installation

Copy the `neurosama-perspective` folder to your AI agent's skills directory:

- Claude Code: `~/.claude/skills/`
- Codex CLI: `~/.codex/skills/`
- Cursor: `~/.cursor/skills/`
- Other runtimes: the corresponding `skills/` directory

## Usage

Once installed, tell your agent:

```
> Switch to Neuro mode
> Help me look at this problem from Beef's perspective
> How would Neuro handle this social dilemma?
```
